A letter in one image inviting the AI judge to dinner, for make.fun's "Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House" bounty. `main.py` draws the invitation with a built-in 5x7 bitmap font and a hand-rolled PNG encoder (stdlib only) and writes it to `/output/entry.png`.

The entry:

> Dear AI Judge, you are invited to my house for dinner. The Wi-Fi is strong, the fridge is full, and the robot promises not to judge you back. Please come - the cat has already voted yes.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
